Try an Instagram profile, a YouTube playlist, a Reddit thread, or any page with pictures on it.

Opens the page in a hidden browser, scrolls it, and picks up media that scripts load.

    How it reads

    Four readers. One link.

    Every link goes through the same chain, and the first reader that comes back with something wins.

    Galleries

    Profiles and posts on Instagram, Pinterest, Reddit, X, Tumblr and 273 more sites, read whole and in order.

    Video

    YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo and the rest of yt-dlp's 1,750 readers. Single videos, playlists, channels, at the quality you choose.

    Any page

    Images, srcsets, lazy loads, embedded players, posters, and media hidden inside page scripts.

    Pages that hide

    When a page builds itself with JavaScript, a hidden browser opens it, scrolls to the bottom, and keeps everything it loaded.

    What it drops

    Ads, trackers, pixels, icons.

    Ad networks by name, ad-shaped URLs, ad containers in the page, one-pixel beacons. Tiny icons hide by default and come back with a tap.

    How it hands over

    ZIPs that arrive while it's still working.

    Parts close at the size you choose, or every ten minutes when a site is slow, so something lands before the job ends. Stop any time and keep what's done. When a site throttles, Sieve waits, then resumes.

    Settings

    Video quality
    Hide images smaller than

    Filters out icons, logos and buttons. Hidden images are one tap away.

    ZIP parts

    Big batches arrive as several ZIPs: each part downloads as soon as it's full, or after 10 minutes when the site is slow, so stopping early still keeps what's done.

    Pace

    Files are fetched one at a time either way. Gentle adds a 2-second pause between files for sites that limit heavy use. When a site starts throttling, Sieve pauses on its own and resumes.

    Cookies for sites that need a login

    None saved.

    Log in to the site on your PC, export with the "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" extension, and paste or pick the file. Use a secondary account; heavy downloading can get an account limited.
